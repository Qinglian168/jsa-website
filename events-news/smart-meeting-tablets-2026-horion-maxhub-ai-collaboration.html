The $7.44 Billion Smart Display Revolution

The global interactive flat panel display (IFPD) market has reached $7.44 billion in 2026, growing at a CAGR of 9.07% and projected to hit $16.26 billion by 2035, according to Business Research Insights. This growth is no longer driven by education alone — the enterprise collaboration segment has emerged as the fastest-growing application, with regional growth rates approaching twice those of the education sector.

RUNTO's 2025 market report confirms the momentum: global large-size interactive flat panel shipments reached 283.5 million units, up 10.7% year-over-year, with overseas markets growing 15.5% to 154.9 million units and China contributing 128.6 million units. For 2026, RUNTO forecasts total shipments of 291 million units, with a long-term CAGR of 3.7% through 2030.

Five AI Trends Reshaping Meetings in 2026

The smart meeting tablet market's growth is propelled by fundamental shifts in how AI transforms collaboration. According to Owl Labs' 2026 State of Hybrid Work report, 80% of workers now use AI tools at work (up 19% from early 2025), and 65% of global enterprises run hybrid work models. Yet 67% of workers have abandoned trying to set up video technology because it was too difficult, and 77% have lost time due to meetings starting late from technical difficulties. AI is closing this gap.

1. Agentic AI Workflows

Zoom's AI Companion 3.0 (March 2026) and Microsoft Copilot have evolved from simple transcribers into active meeting participants. They proactively surface relevant files when topics are mentioned, live-tag action items to specific owners, draft follow-up emails, schedule next meetings, and update project trackers. Zoom reports AI Companion MAU tripled year-over-year in Q4 FY26. Meeting workload is reduced by over 50%.

2. Intelligent AV Hardware Replacing Peripherals

Multi-camera “Director AI” systems automatically find the best angle for every person in the room, eliminating the “laptop lean” problem. Horion's four-camera architecture and MAXHUB's triple-camera matrix with 5x hybrid zoom represent this shift. Auto-framing, eye-contact correction, and exposure correction are now default expectations, not premium add-ons.

3. Real-Time Multilingual Translation

Global teams now converse in their native tongues with sub-second, voice-cloned translation that maintains the speaker's original tone. Horion's 99% accuracy transcription and MAXHUB's Lingxiao AI model both support real-time translation. This capability has become table stakes for multinational enterprises with distributed teams across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and the Americas.

4. Meeting Equity as Standard

Meeting equity — ensuring remote participants feel equally present, heard, and engaged — has moved from aspirational goal to baseline requirement. AI automatically balances lighting, suppresses home office background noise, and uses spatial audio to place voices in virtual space. If a colleague on the left side of the screen speaks, their voice physically sounds like it's coming from the left, reducing “meeting fatigue” by mimicking real-world sound processing.

5. BYOM: Bring Your Own Meeting

The battle between “Zoom Rooms” and “Teams Rooms” has been settled by interoperability. Modern smart meeting tablets are platform-agnostic: employees walk in, plug in a single USB-C cable, and host high-definition meetings on Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Webex, or Google Meet — all using the room's professional-grade cameras and microphones with zero configuration. MAXHUB's native Teams Rooms certification and Horion's Google EDLA certification exemplify this trend.
